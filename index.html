Chicken Cobbler
Rotisserie chicken
Ingredients: Rotisserie chicken (Sam's) · Frozen mixed veggies · Cheddar Bay biscuit mix · Cream of chicken soup (pantry) · Chicken broth · Butter
Method: Layer butter on bottom of baking dish · Add shredded chicken + veggies · Mix soup + broth and pour over · Top with Cheddar Bay biscuit mix + water · Bake 375°F 45-55 min until golden
Side: Bagged salad

Tuesday
June 9
Oyster Mushroom Orzotto
🍄 Meatless
Ingredients: Fresh foraged oyster mushrooms · Orzo pasta · Butter · Onion · Minced garlic · White wine · Chicken broth · Grated Parmesan · Salt & pepper
Method: Sauté mushrooms in butter until golden · Set aside · Sauté onion + garlic · Add orzo and toast 2 min · Add white wine, stir until absorbed · Add broth one ladle at a time stirring like risotto · Fold mushrooms back in · Finish with Parmesan and butter
Side: Frozen broccoli
Wednesday
June 10
Venison Stew Meat Stir Fry
Venison stew meat
Ingredients: Venison stew meat · Aldi teriyaki sauce · California blend veggies · Rice · Minced garlic · Butter or oil
Method: Cook rice · Sear venison in hot cast iron until browned · Add garlic + veggies · Pour in teriyaki sauce · Toss and cook until veggies are tender · Serve over rice
Side: California blend veggies mixed in
🧊 5:00 PM — Pull Easter ham from freezer for Thursday
Thursday
June 11
Instant Pot Ham Mac & Cheese
Easter ham
Ingredients: 16 oz elbow macaroni · 4 tbsp melted butter · 4 cups water · 1¼ cups evaporated milk · 2 cups shredded cheddar · 8 oz Velveeta cut into pieces · Diced Easter ham
Method: Combine pasta, water, butter in Instant Pot · High pressure 5 min · Quick release · Stir in cheeses + evaporated milk until melted · Fold in diced ham · Use sauté mode if cheese needs more heat
Side: Peas
