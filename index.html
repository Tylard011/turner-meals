November 8
Outback-Style Potato Soup + Good Bread + Wilted Greens
🥣 Crockpot
Soup: 30 oz bag frozen shredded hash browns + 3 cans (14 oz) chicken broth + 1 can cream of chicken soup + ½ cup chopped onion + ¼ tsp pepper · crockpot on low 6–8 hours (start around 10–11 AM) · stir in 1 package softened full-fat cream cheese for the last hour
Stovetop: Simmer until the potatoes are soft, then add the cream cheese · about 1 hour total
Lumps: Soften the cream cheese and whisk it with a ladle of hot soup before stirring it in
Toppings: Shredded cheddar · sour cream · bacon bits · green onions
Wilted greens: Garlic in a little oil or bacon fat · add the kale with a splash of chicken broth and a pinch of salt · cover 3–5 minutes · finish with lemon or vinegar
Side: Good bread

Crockpot Chicken & Stuffing + Frozen Green Beans
🍗 Chicken breast🥘 Crockpot
Layer: 4 thawed boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 2.5 lbs) in a single layer · 2 cans cream of chicken soup · swirl in ½ cup sour cream · ¼ cup diced onion + 1 Tbsp dried parsley
Topping: 6 oz box chicken stuffing mix tossed with ¼ cup melted butter
Cook: Low 6–7 hours or high 3–4 hours · uncover the last 45 min if you want the stuffing firmer
Note: Chicken must be thawed, not frozen
Side: Frozen green beans

Venison stew meat🔥 Sous Vide + Cast Iron
